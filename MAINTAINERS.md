Maintainers are added and removed as [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#the-contributor-ladder)
describes: a reviewer who meets the maintainer criteria is nominated in a
public issue, and the existing maintainers decide. Former maintainers are
listed under Emeritus once there are any.

For a vulnerability or a conduct report, use the private routes in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#reporting),
never a public issue.
